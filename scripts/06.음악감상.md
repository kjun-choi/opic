## 묘사 ##

나는 오아시스 팬이야. 몇 십년 전부터 그들은 유명했지.
그들은 2 형제로 만들어졌는데 둘이 매일 싸우고, 심지어 깨졌었어.
그라나 최근에 화해 (make up) 했고, 그 밴드는 reunited 되었어.

I'm a big fan of the British rock band Oasis. Even though they became famous decades ago, they're still legendary.
The band consists of two brothers two brothers who used to fight all the time, which eventually led to their breakup. But recently they've made up each other and eventually the band has been reunited. I was so excited to hear the news, and I can't wait for their new songs.

## 시제 ##

어렸을 때는 외국 노래 접하기 힘들어.
MTV 본적 있는데 그 때 오아시스 노래 처음 들음
나는 충격 받고, 그들 노래를 찾고 모으기 시작함.
작년에 그 재결합 소식을 알렸고, 한국에서 공연한다 함.
거기 정말 가고 싶었지만, 실패. 나는 여전히 귿르의 노래를 들으며 살아.

When I was a kid, it wasn't easy to access pop music. It was too expensive to buy an album of the foriegn artists, and I didn't know much about them. 
Back then, I happened to watch MTV, it was a first time for me to listen to Oasis's song. I was given a shot and I started listening and collecting their music.
Last year, when they announced their reunion, they planned a concert in Korea. I really wanted to go, but I failed to get tickets. Still, I always enjoy listening to their music whenever I'm on the move.

## 과거와 비교 ##

어렸을 때 나는 항상 노래 들으며 살았어. 그 때는 iPod 를 항상 들고 다녔지.
그러나 나는 이제 흥미를 잃엇어. 내가 알던 가수나 밴들들은 은퇴했거나 이제 음악활동을 하지 않아. 심지어 어떤 가수는 죽었어. 이게 내가 오아시스 컴백에 더 훙분한 이유 같아.

When I was young, I always used to listen to music. Back then I carried my iPod everywhere to listen to music. 
However, as I got older, I lost interest in music. The singers or bands I used to like  either retired or stopped making new music. Some artists have even passed away. Maybe that’s why I’m even more excited about Oasis’s comeback.
