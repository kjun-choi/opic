내 머리를 자르고 머리를 감겨주었어.

Recently, I visited the hair salon to get my hair cut. As usual, I had my regular hairstylist take care of my hair. I usually prefer a neat and clean look, so I didn't change my style much.
While I was waiting for my hairstylist, they kindly served me a drink. When it was my turn, they recieved my jacket and gave me the gown (cape). I got my hair cut and they gave me a refreshing hair wash. I felt relaxed and comfortable, that is why I go there regularly.
