## 묘사 ##
한국은 엄격한 재활용 시스템 가지고 있음.
특히, 우리 아파트는 재활용을 위한 특별한 area 있음.
플라스틱, 캔, 종이, 음식물 쓰레기 나누어 넣음.
우리는 자주 배달음식 주문하고, 온라인 쇼핑 즐겨. 
우리는 플라스틱 container 와 carboard box 자주 나와.
cotainer plastic wash out 하고 box flatten 해.

Well, in Korea, we have a very strict recycling system. Especially in my apartment complex, there is a specific area for recycling.
At home, I usually separate trash into plastics, cans, paper, and food waste. You know, my family orders delivery food quite often and does a lot of online shopping. So, we always have tons of plastic containers and cardboard boxes. I have to wash out the containers and flatten the boxes before throwing them away.

## 과거 ##

나는 포기하고 출근했는데, 점심쯤 비가 멈추가 내 아내가 그것들을 버렸어.
나는 고마워서 아내를 took 해서 dinner 먹음.

My apartment has a recycling rule that is people should throw out the recycling trash on Mondays. One day, it rained heavliy on that day. I hesitated to carry them to the recycling area. Finally, I gave up throwing them out and I went to work. It stopped raining around lunch as a result, my wife had to do it instead of me. I appreciated my wife and took my wife out for dinner.

나도 그 룰을 따르고 집에도 빈드이 있어.

Compared to the past, these days people do a lot more online shopping and ordering delivery food has become part of daily life. Because of this, tons of trash end up piling up at home. In my apartment, we have to separate everything—like plastic, paper, cans, and stuff like that. So I always follow the rules and keep separate recycling bins at home.
