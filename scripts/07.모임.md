2. 최근 기억에 남는 모임 (시제)
I go out for dinner about once a month with my teammates.
We usually go eat Korean BBQ, One day, one of my teammates suggested going to the seafood market. In fact, there's a huge seafood market near our company, but we had never been there.
Everyone liked the idea and we went to the market. 
We bought fresh tuna sashimi and king crab at a really good price. It was very delicious and I decided to visit the market with my family.
