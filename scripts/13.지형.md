Korea is a peninsula which is surrounded by water on three sides.
Especially, Korea is well known for a lot of seafood. These days, a lot of foreigners visit Korea and head straight to fish markets. They really like to try raw seafood, like live octopus.
And there are many moutains in Korea. Korea is covered by about 70% of mountains.
I think Korea is one of the most beautiful countries in the world and I love my country.

## 특정 지형 ##
속초는 나의 최애 중 하나.
속초는 집에서 2-3시간이면 감.
속초는 바다와 설악산 유명해. -> 나는 오전에 산가고 저녁에 seafood 먹는거 좋아해.
속초는 유명한 관광지, 사람들 많이 감. 나도 쉬거나 재충전하러 자주 가.

속초 is one of my favorite cities in Korea. It's located on the east coast, it takes two or three hours by car from my place. The place's especially well known for the beautiful ocean view and Mt. Seorak. Whenever I go there, I usually go hiking Mt. Seorak during the day and then enjoy fresh seafood in the evening.
Sokcho is a very popular tourist city, so a lot of people visit. I also like going there to relax and recharge.
