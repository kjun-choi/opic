## 묘사 ##

한국은 4계절이 있음.
봄과 가을이 요즘 너무 짧음
여름이 너무 더워서 쉽게 잘 지침
그래서 많은 사람들이 집에 에어컨과 함께 지냄.

Well, Korea has four seasons: spring, summer, fall, and winter.
But honestly, spring and fall are very short these days.
The summer is really hot and humid, and winter is freezing cold.
Especially in the summer, I get tired easily because of the humidity.
So, most people just stay indoors with the air conditioning.

## 좋아하는 계절 ##

나는 가을 제일 좋아함.
덥지도 춥지도 않음. 날씨가 미쳤음
저녁에 시원한 breeze 기분 좋게 함.
저녁 산책-> 스트레스 풀림

I love fall the most.
You know, it is not too hot and not too cold. The weather is just perfect.
In the evening, the cool breeze feels so nice.
After a busy workday, walking in this weather really helps me relieve stress.
That's why fall is my favorite season.
