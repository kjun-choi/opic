## 묘사 ##

깔끔하고, 단정한거 좋아함
개발자라 다른 사람 시선 신경 안 씀
회사는 청바지, 셔츠 입음
** 그러나 집에서는 편하게 입음 (sweatpants) **

I usually prefer a neat, smart casual look.
I don't really care about what others think of my outfits. As a software engineer, I rarely meet outside clients, and I mostly work with the same teammates.
When I go to work, I usually wear jeans paired with a collared shirt to keep it clean and neat.
But I am at home, Comfort is king. I usually wear a T-shirt and sweatpants at home every day.  

## 과거/현재 비교 ##
나 많이 변했음.
예전에는 타이트한 옷 많이 입음.
그러니 지금은 깔끔하고 캐주얼한 look 추구함.
딸 있음, 나보다 그녀 더 신경씀

To be honest, my style has changed a lot.
I was pretty skinny before. So, back then I used to wear the tight-fitting clothes to follow trends. But now, I prefer a neat and casual look. I like wearing a shirt, jeans and sneakers. Since I have a little girl, I care much more about her outfits than mine.

## 옷 관련 과거 경험 ##
삿포로 여자친구와 여행감.
생각보다 추웠음 -> 그래서 옷가게 가서 자켓이랑 스웨터 (sweatshirts) 삼
지금도 추워지면 그 옷 잘 입음.

I went on a trip to Sapporo with my girl friend a few years ago. 
It's located in the northern part of Japan. When we arrived at the airport, it was way colder than we expected.  So, we had no choice but to drop by a store and buy warm winter jackets and sweatshirts. 
Thanks to those clothes, we stayed warm and thoroughly enjoyed our trip.
I still have the clothes and sometimes wear them when it gets chilly.
